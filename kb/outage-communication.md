# Outage Communication Protocol

When a platform component fails widely enough to affect bookings in a city, the incident commander declares an outage and support switches to outage mode within 10 minutes. A holding message is published on the in-app status banner and on the official social handles first, then a single agreed wording is pushed to every support channel so that no two agents describe the incident differently. Updates go out every 30 minutes until service is restored, even when there is nothing new to report, and agents must not speculate about the cause or promise a restoration time that engineering has not confirmed. Tickets raised during the outage window are tagged with the incident identifier so they can be reviewed together and compensated in one batch afterwards.
