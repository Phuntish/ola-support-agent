======================================================================
TASK 6 - TICKET LOOKUP AND ESCALATION SCORE
======================================================================
Formula
  escalation_score = 0.55 * escalated + 0.45 * min(days_since_created / 30, 1)
  range [0, 1]: fresh and unflagged = 0.0, flagged and 30 days old = 1.0

Threshold, derived from this dataset
  p80 of days_since_created over 60 tickets = 20.20 days
  score of an unflagged ticket at that age  = 0.45 * 20.20/30 = 0.303
  ESCALATION_THRESHOLD = 0.303

What that threshold actually selects
  score range across the dataset : 0.0000 - 0.9250
  lowest score of a flagged ticket : 0.5950
  highest score of an unflagged one: 0.4350
  tickets at or above threshold  : 19/60 = 31.7%
    - carrying escalated=True    : 10
    - stale but never escalated  : 9   <- what a bare boolean OR would miss
